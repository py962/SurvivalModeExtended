# Changelog

## 1.0.0-beta.1

First public beta, for Minecraft 1.21.11 and NeoForge 21.11.42 or newer. Requires Curios API 14.0.0 or newer. Start a new world.

### Highlights

- Body temperature, energy and a reworked food system with five purposes.
- Seasons with changing day length, seasonal rain and snow, and sandstorms.
- Villages in three sizes, villager comfort and moods, reputation and paired trading.
- Four travelling guides with quest chains that lead to structures across the world.
- New structures: the Outlaws Hideout, the Trappers Lodge and the Pirates Cove.
- Eight Celestial conduits and amulets, made at the Celestial Forge under an aurora.
- New creatures, biomes, enchantments, potion effects, shields, arrows and ambience.

### Known issues

- Balance is still being tuned; temperatures, prices and spawn rates may change between betas.
- Worlds created with this beta may not upgrade cleanly to later versions. Keep backups.
