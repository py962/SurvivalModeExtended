## Gallery images (both sites)

Aim for 4 to 8 screenshots, 16:9, without the debug screen or chat:

- A village at dusk, villagers gathered (the singing).
- An aurora over a snowy landscape, ideally with the Celestial Forge lit.
- The marsh with morning mist.
- A sandstorm in the badlands.
- The Outlaws Hideout interior, or its tunnel mouth.
- A guide with its donkey.
- The new creatures (owls, geese, vultures, caribou, elephant with howdah).
- The temperature HUD in a cold or hot biome.
