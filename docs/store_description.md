**Survival Mode Extended** is a survival overhaul that makes the world something to prepare for and live in, while keeping the look and feel of vanilla.

> **Beta.** Expect bugs and balance changes, and keep backups of any world you use it in. Please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/py962/SurvivalModeExtended/issues).

## Survive the weather

- **Body temperature** follows the biome, the season, the time of day, altitude, shelter, weather and nearby heat sources. Dress for the cold, find shade in the desert, and carry a thermometer.
- **Energy** drains with effort and is restored by the right food.
- **Food** has five purposes: health, cooling, warmth, energy and hunger.

## A living year

- **Seasons** change the length of the day, the temperature, and whether it rains or snows.
- **Sandstorms** sweep the deserts and badlands, leaving drifts of sand behind them.
- **Auroras** light up cold nights.
- **Beacons** can be paid to call up a thunderstorm or clear the sky.

## Villages worth caring for

- **Small, medium and large villages**, more common than in vanilla.
- **Villagers show how they feel:** cold, hot, fearful, tired, trapped or hungry, readable from posture and voice.
- **Village tiers and reputation:** look after a village and watch it grow. At dusk, a thriving village sings.
- **Paired trading:** every trade has a matching buy and sell, so reputation pays off without letting you farm emeralds.

## Guides and their quests

Four travelling guides, the **Archeologist**, **Ranger**, **Mariner** and **Priest**, each with a chain of quests that sends you across the world to find relics.

## New places to find

- **Outlaws Hideout**, buried deep in a badlands hill, with a front tunnel for riders and a rail escape tunnel.
- **Trappers Lodge**, hidden in the snowy forests, with a secret cellar.
- **Pirates Cove** on the coast.

## Celestial conduits and amulets

Eight paired conduits and amulets (Breath, Life, Death, Shield, Peace, Eye, Luck and End), crafted at the **Celestial Forge**, which only wakes under an aurora. Wear the amulet, build the conduit, and pair them.

## New creatures

Owls, geese, vultures, caribou, grizzly bears, elephants (ride one in a howdah), granite golems, ghost fish and the Frostbitten.

## And more

- New biomes: marsh, frozen marsh, inland rivers and ice caves, with morning mist and river rapids.
- New enchantments, potion effects and tipped arrows.
- Wooden shields, feather-tiered arrows, the fur cloak, the leather flask and the houseboat.
- The Ancient Codex for enchanting your own books, and the Replicator for building blocks.
- Layered biome ambience that responds to altitude, weather and the ocean.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.11** with **NeoForge 21.11.42** or newer.
- **[Curios API](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) 14.0.0** or newer.
- Install on **both the client and the server**.
- **Start a new world.** Much of the mod changes world generation.
