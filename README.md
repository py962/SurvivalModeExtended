# Survival Mode Extended

A survival overhaul for **Minecraft 1.21.11** and **26.3** on **NeoForge**. It makes the world something to prepare for and live in, while keeping the look and feel of vanilla.

> **Beta.** Expect bugs and balance changes, and keep backups of any world you use it in. Please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/py962/SurvivalModeExtended/issues).

## Features

- **Survival:** body temperature driven by biome, season, time of day, altitude, shelter, weather and heat sources; an energy bar; reworked hunger and food with five purposes (health, cooling, warmth, energy, hunger).
- **Seasons:** a yearly cycle with changing day length, seasonal temperatures and rain, snow, and sandstorms in deserts and badlands. Beacons can call up a storm or clear the sky.
- **Villages:** small, medium and large villages; villager comfort and moods you can read from their posture; village tiers and reputation; paired buy-and-sell trading that rewards reputation without allowing emerald farming; villagers who sing at dusk.
- **Guides:** four travelling guides (Archeologist, Ranger, Mariner and Priest) with quest chains that lead to structures across the world.
- **Structures:** the Outlaws Hideout buried in the badlands, the Trappers Lodge in the snowy forests, and the Pirates Cove on the coast, each with its own raiders.
- **Celestial conduits and amulets:** eight paired conduits and amulets, crafted at the Celestial Forge under an aurora, with effects tied to their themes.
- **New creatures:** owls, geese, vultures, caribou, grizzly bears, elephants (with a howdah), granite golems, ghost fish and the Frostbitten.
- **World:** new biomes (marsh, frozen marsh, inland rivers, ice caves), morning mist over the marshes, river rapids, and auroras in the cold.
- **Items and effects:** new enchantments, potion effects and tipped arrows, wooden shields, feather-tiered arrows, the fur cloak, the leather flask, the houseboat, the Ancient Codex, the Replicator and more.
- **Ambience:** layered biome soundscapes that blend between biomes and respond to altitude, weather and the ocean.

- - For further details see https://survivalmodeextended.substack.com

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.11
- NeoForge 21.11.42 or newer
- [Curios API](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) 14.0.0 or newer
- Install on both the client and the server.
- Start a new world: much of the mod changes world generation.

## Building from source

Requires Java 21 (25 for 26.3)

```
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar is written to `build/libs/`.
